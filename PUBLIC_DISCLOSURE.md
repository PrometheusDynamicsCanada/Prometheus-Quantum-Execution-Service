# Public Disclosure & Intellectual Property Boundary

This repository archives sanitized empirical hardware benchmarks, pre-execution SHA-256 commitment manifests, dated pre-holdout amendment logs, per-workload and per-rung measured hardware outcomes (`fb_measured`, `fh_measured`, `tvd_measured`, `shannon_entropy_bits`, `observable_measured`), cluster specifications, and provider-billed seconds/credits across **157 provider records (149 completed benchmark jobs + 4 scheduled station warm-ups + 4 troubleshooting records = 2,509 completed physical PUBs, 2,093,568 completed physical shots, 544.00 s IBM QPU time, 399.75 credits IQM Resonance)** executed across **308 disjoint unseen logical workloads** (96 in Phase 1/2 + 212 in Phase 3) on **IBM Quantum Heron (`ibm_kingston`, `ibm_fez`, `ibm_marrakesh` 156Q)** and **IQM Resonance (`IQM Emerald` 54Q & `IQM Garnet` 20Q)**.

## 1. Withheld Proprietary Methodology & Recorded Commitments
In accordance with Prometheus Dynamics' intellectual property boundary policy across all public repositories:
1. **Compiler & Subgraph Selection (`subgraph_sha256` & `sub_B*_sha256` Recorded Commitments):** Internal multi-objective scoring weights, subgraph ranking formulas, routing heuristics, and raw physical qubit coordinate mappings (`QB1..QB54` and Heron physical indices) are withheld. Across all Phase 3 frozen plans (`data/phase3_exp*_preexecution_frozen_*.json`) and `data/phase2b_calibration_factorial_results.json`, raw physical subgraph coordinate lists are replaced with deterministic SHA-256 subgraph commitment hashes (`subgraph_sha256` and `sub_B*_sha256`). External auditors can verify pre-execution subgraph commitments, buffer distance diagnostics (`realized_min_d_G`), and matching recorded subgraph commitments (`sub_B2_live_sha256 == sub_B4_live_enforced_equal_B2_sha256`, `jaccard_B2_vs_B4 = 1.0`); establishing the underlying physical execution regions on the QPU requires the withheld coordinate mapping.
2. **Prospective Execution Scheduler, Refresh Triggers & Live-Stopping Rules:** Internal prospective feature weights, duration-grouping threshold equations, numerical fallback triggers, empirical billing formulas, internal survival-model predictions (`c1_fpred_live`, `c2_fpred_live`, `predicted_gains`), internal policy ablations, and per-workload candidate-origin routing labels (`selected_compiler`, `compiler_selected`) are withheld (`REDACTED_PROPRIETARY_SCHEDULER_RULE_PER_PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE`). Where a public ledger is sanitized, its pre-sanitization SHA-256 digest is recorded in `"original_private_ledger_sha256"` to preserve an internal reference to the private original across files (without claiming to independently authenticate withheld private files on its own in the public release); local JSON timestamp fields similarly record benchmark runner serialization times rather than third-party pre-submission notarization.
3. **Proprietary Source Implementations & Clean Repository Release:** Internal compiler passes and execution-service heuristic engines are excluded from this public archive, which is published as a freshly created single-commit sanitized repository (`0` historical pre-sanitization commits in repository object storage; external copies downloaded prior to repository recreation cannot be recalled).
